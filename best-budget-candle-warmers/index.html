Candle warmers let you enjoy your favorite scents without an open flame, great for homes with kids, pets, or anyone who’s forgetful about blowing out wicks. They gently heat the wax from above or below, releasing fragrance more evenly and making candles last longer than burning alone. When shopping for a warmer, consider whether you want a plug-in model that fits over a single candle, a lamp style with adjustable height and brightness, or a plate that can heat multiple types of containers.

Quick answer

The Candle Warmers Etc. Full-Size Illumination Fragrance Warmer is the best all-around pick for its even heat and attractive design.

If you prefer a compact ceramic warmer for melts, the Airom Electric Ceramic Wax Melt Warmer is a close runner-up, while the Hosley bowl is ideal for those who exclusively use wax melts.

Candle warmers let you enjoy your favorite scents without an open flame, meaningless soot, no fire risk, and a longer-lasting fragrance throw. They gently heat the wax from above or below, releasing aroma slowly and safely, which makes them a popular alternative to burning candles, especially in homes with pets or kids. When choosing a warmer, consider the heat type: top-down lamp warmers preserve the candle jar and work with both candles and melts, while bottom-up plate or bowl warmers are better for melts alone. Also look at size, style, and whether you want adjustable heat or a built-in timer. The options below cover everything from budget-friendly plug-ins to premium dimmable lamps.
